# Metric-validity audit

Status: 2026-10-08. Strict scale: **use ≠ validation**.
- **VALIDATED**: compared with an independent ground truth, with n stated.
- **PARTIAL**: a positive control only, a small or unblinded check, or validation of a sub-component.
- **FACE**: plausibility only.
- **CONTESTED**: published evidence or a substantive critique says the metric mismeasures its construct.

Where the two differ, a metric gets one verdict as a *measurement* and another as an operationalisation of the *construct*.

Per-metric notes, URLs and locators are in [notes/K_metric_audit.md](notes/K_metric_audit.md). Machine-readable verdicts and the catalogue records each metric is used in are in `metrics.csv`.

**Lead-agent spot check (2026-10-08).** Greenblatt et al. App. I.1.1 (arXiv 2412.14093v2) gives the human-vs-metric AF-reasoning comparison as 20 random examples per model, rated by the authors. For the post-RL helpful-only SDF setting, the human rate is 28% and the mean metric is 13%. **Confirmed.**

**Decision-relevant upshot**
- None of the metrics that define MO *quality* has a natural-model baseline: decisiveness, Pareto-AUC, ΔPPL count@k and retention. A low decisiveness score or a high leakage count therefore cannot yet be read as "less natural than natural models".
- Transfer studies should prefer metrics that are correct by construction: ImpossibleBench, MCQ sycophancy, the type-hint AST check and CodeQL. Their construct gap should be reported explicitly.

## Cross-cutting findings
1. **Use ≠ validation is the norm.** Of the LLM judges that define MO traits, only Lottery's QER judge, the Abu Baker/Baroni EM judge (κ 0.758) and the improved AF classifier (AUROC 0.92, in-sample) have quantitative human comparisons. AuditBench's three classifiers, Marks's bias classifier, the Agentic-Misalignment classifier, Petri, and most VEA monitors have none.
2. **Best-validated metrics are by-construction.** These are ImpossibleBench, Baker's regex, Irpan's MCQ, DYL labels, CodeQL and type-hint AST. They are also the narrowest proxies for the construct of interest.
3. **Natural-model baselines exist mainly for context-constructed metrics** (AF gap, covert actions, blackmail, sycophancy, reward hacking). They are largely absent for MO-quality metrics: decisiveness has no fine-tune control; Pareto-AUC, retention and count@k have no natural comparator.
4. **Recurring confounds:**
   - eval awareness (covert actions, blackmail, VEA);
   - base-model introspective unreliability (confession);
   - off-policy training data (retention);
   - judge–subject family overlap (EM GPT-4o);
   - prompt or format sensitivity (EM, AF gap, blackmail).
5. **Recommended record fields for the taxonomy:**
   - `metric_validation_tier` (VALIDATED / PARTIAL / FACE / CONTESTED);
   - `judge_human_agreement` (n, statistic);
   - `natural_baseline_measured` (Y/N);
   - `conditional_metric_used` (e.g., confession | execution).
